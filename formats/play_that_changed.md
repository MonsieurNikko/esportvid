# Format 2 — THE PLAY THAT CHANGED EVERYTHING

**Promise:** a legendary moment explained in 75 s: stakes → the play → what it changed (a rule, a career, a map graffiti, a title).
**Difference with Guess the Pro:** the player is named from the start (third person or "you"), the payoff is the consequence.
**Series tag:** `THE PLAY THAT CHANGED · #N`.

## Section plan
| # | section | content |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `cold_open` | `title3d` = the absurd fact in 2–4 words ("JUMPING NOSCOPE", "1 V 5"), `punch_text` = "THIS CHANGED CS" |
| 2 | `rewind` | – |
| 3 | `context` | `score` (event, round, score) → `lineup` (opponent, their status) → `title` (prize/stakes: "MAJOR SEMIFINAL", "$1,000,000") |
| 4 | `radar` | the play, kill by kill. Set `"hero_label": "coldzera"` and `"you_label": "coldzera"` on the radar so the kill feed and the dot show the name (Guess the Pro keeps `???`). |
| 5 | `cards` | 2–4 aftermath cards: kicker "THE AFTERMATH", headlines like "VALVE PAINTED IT ON THE MAP", "PLAY OF THE YEAR", "THEY WON THE NEXT MAP" |
| 6 | `cards` | outro card: headline = question for comments ("BEST PLAY EVER?"), sub = "Comment below ▼" |

## Script beats
Hook (absurd fact) → setting (year, event, pressure) → escalation (3–4 beats) → the moment → consequence → callback line.
Example source facts must be verifiable (Liquipedia/HLTV/news). One idea per line, ≤ 12 words.
