# Format 1 — GUESS THE PRO

**Promise:** "Can you name the player from the play?" Second-person story ("you"), answer hidden until the end.
**Why it works:** open loop from second 1, comment bait ("comment your guess before 3"), rewatch loop.
**Length:** 70–80 s (TikTok Creator Rewards needs > 60 s).

## Section plan
| # | section | content | voice (example) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `cold_open` | hero + N opponents, `title3d` = "1 V 4" | "One versus four. Fifty HP. No armor." + punch "Guess who." |
| 2 | `rewind` | – | – |
| 3 | `context` | `score` (date/event chip) → `lineup` (opponents + 1–2 facts) → `title` (what a win gives) → `alarm` (what a loss costs) | "Round thirty. You lead fifteen to fourteen." / "Your opponent: …" / "Win the series, and you go to…" / "Lose this round, and it's overtime." |
| 4 | `radar` | team rush → deaths → objective → 1vX kill by kill → stats | short lines between the kills, names said as they die |
| 5 | `clue` | the easiest clue (nickname, signature weapon, catchphrase) | "One last clue. …" + big text |
| 6 | `reveal` | question, 3-2-1, answer + team · event · year | "So, who are you? Comment your guess before three." / "It's …" / "Got it right? Comment below." |

## Script rules
- Never say or show the answer before the reveal (no hero name on the radar: the kill feed shows `???`).
- Clues go from hard to easy: context (event, opponents) → map/weapon → nickname. Opponent names are allowed (they hook fans).
- Every claim must be in `sources` (HLTV/vlr.gg/Liquipedia). If the analysis doesn't say it, don't narrate it
  (e.g. "two shots, no kill" — not "two misses" when the victim was damaged).
- Stakes must be exact: "win the series → LAN" ≠ "win the round → LAN". Write the real consequence.

## Titles (publish)
`[what happened] [game]`: "kennyS 1v4 with 50 HP vs NiP — CS:GO clutch" · "This 1v4 sent Titan to Kyiv (CS:GO)".
