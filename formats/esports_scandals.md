# Format 3 — ESPORTS SCANDALS (dark mood)

**Promise:** the true story of a cheat, exploit, match-fixing or forfeit, told like a thriller.
Set `"mood": "dark"` (red accent, uneasy drone, glitch on cards). Series tag: `ESPORTS SCANDALS · #N`.

## Section plan
| # | section | content |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `cold_open` | `enemies` = 0–2, `title3d` = the verdict word ("BANNED", "FORFEIT", "PIXEL BOOST"), `punch_text` = "IT WAS A LIE." / "THEY KNEW." |
| 2 | `rewind` | – |
| 3 | `context` | `score` (event + date) → `lineup` (the team involved) → `title` (what was at stake) |
| 4 | `radar` (optional) | show the exploit spot / the round where it happened (pulse a `zones` rect on the spot) |
| 5 | `cards` | the investigation: "THE CLIP", "THE RULING", "THE PUNISHMENT", "WHAT HAPPENED NEXT" |
| 6 | `cards` | outro: "WAS IT FAIR?" + "Comment below ▼" (debate = comments) |

## Legal / platform safety (mandatory)
- Only documented facts with sources (official rulings, Valve/organizer statements, ESIC, major outlets). Quote verdicts, not rumours.
- Neutral verbs: "was banned by", "the organizer ruled", "forfeited". No insults, no new accusations, no private individuals.
- YouTube: no "repetitive disturbing content" — the story must have a clear narrative and a factual payoff.
